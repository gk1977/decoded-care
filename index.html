Why this book

Plans that work on paper. And plans that work for people.

Medicare Advantage is one of the great operating achievements in American healthcare. Running a plan well is also one of the hardest jobs in it. The same mistakes repeat at scale: plans that pencil out on paper but quietly drift toward insolvency, physicians worn down by incentives that point the wrong way, members whose experience erodes one operational shortcut at a time.

None of it comes from malice. It comes from reasonable decisions made in isolation. Decoded.Care translates what actually works into a playbook, one that treats operational excellence as moral work and holds that the people Medicare Advantage serves have earned better than cleverness.

One chapter, three lenses

Written for everyone who sits at the table.

Every chapter follows the same decision through three sets of eyes, because in Medicare Advantage a choice made in the finance office lands in the exam room and, eventually, at a member's kitchen table.

The plan lens

Healthcare leaders

How the money really moves: revenue, risk adjustment, medical cost, Star Ratings, distribution, and the trade-offs behind every line of a plan P&L.

Followed through Mark, a health plan executive.
The clinical lens

Clinicians

What plan decisions look like from the exam room: networks, prior authorization, value-based contracts, and incentives that help or hurt good care.

Followed through Dr. Sarna, a practicing physician.
The member lens

Members and families

What it means to choose, use, and depend on a plan: benefits, access, care after a hospital stay, and your rights when the answer is no.

Followed through Gloria, a Medicare Advantage member.

The three-legged stool. Financial discipline, clinical trust, and member access. A plan that lets any one leg go short eventually tips over, no matter how strong the other two look.

About the author

Written by an operator.

The author has spent two decades inside Medicare Advantage at every altitude that matters: divisional CFO, chief Medicare officer, and health plan CEO, and an operator again today. A two-time CEO and veteran managed care executive, he wrote the book from the inside, not from the sidelines.

Decoded.Care is his attempt to write down what actually works, so that the next generation of plan leaders, clinicians, and members doesn't have to learn it the expensive way.
